Egg Fried Rice

Ingredients:

  • 2 bowls of cold leftover rice
  • 3 large eggs
  • 3 cloves of garlic
  • 2 stalks of green onions / scallions
  • 2 tablespoons of cooking oil
  • 1 tablespoon light soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon sesame oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon white pepper powder
  • A pinch of salt and chicken powder / MSG

Steps:

  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl, add a tiny pinch of salt and white pepper, and whisk lightly
  2. Heat the cooking oil in a wok or large skillet over medium-high heat. Toss in the minced garlic and the white parts of the green onions. Sauté for about 30 seconds until fragrant
  3. Push the garlic and onions to the side of the wok. Pour the beaten eggs into the empty center. Let the eggs sit and set for about 5 seconds, then quickly scramble them into small, soft curds
  4. Turn the stove up to maximum high heat. Immediately add the cold rice into the wok. Use your spatula to press down and break up any remaining clumps, mixing it thoroughly with the eggs
  5. Drizzle the light soy sauce, sesame oil, remaining white pepper, and salt over the rice. Stir-fry vigorously without stopping for 2–3 minutes. You want the rice to dance and pop in the pan, absorbing the smoky flavor
  6. Toss in the green parts of the green onions. Give it a final, quick toss for 10 seconds, then turn off the heat. Serve hot!
